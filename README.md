# RO'Lyfe Relocation Intelligence Center™

## PA → 50 States

The RO'Lyfe Relocation Intelligence Center is a location-intelligence platform designed to help people research where they may want to live, relocate, invest, build a business, purchase property, or explore new opportunities.

The system starts with Pennsylvania and is designed from day one to expand to all 50 states.

---

# Mission

Instead of asking:

> "What's the best state?"

RO'Lyfe asks:

> "Which location fits the person's actual goals, finances, climate preferences, risk tolerance, housing needs, business plans and opportunity profile?"

The engine is designed to combine multiple datasets and explain the tradeoffs.

---

State → County → City → ZIP → Property
