Tax-Lien Research Workflow

  1. Identify property / parcel.
  2. Identify county and official tax authority.
  3. Verify tax status.
  4. Verify lien amount.
  5. Check redemption rules.
  6. Check expiration / sale dates where applicable.
  7. Verify against official public records.
  8. Document unresolved due-diligence items.
Important Tax-lien information can vary substantially by jurisdiction. RO'Lyfe should treat third-party information as research leads until verified through the applicable official source.
